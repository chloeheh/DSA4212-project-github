\textbf{Summary.} We compare three randomly initialized GPT models under two hours of A10 training time per run. Small performs best. Rising validation losses in medium and large motivate a follow-up with stronger dropout. This improves those models but does not reverse the ranking. We report the unsuccessful change for small, the assumptions behind final testing, and the limits of these single-seed results.
