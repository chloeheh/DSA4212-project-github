# Sources, supported claims, and limits

This reading map records how sources inform the report. Empirical claims rely on peer-reviewed papers where available. Dataset/software specifications use their official documentation. The Smol guide is identified as practitioner advice, not peer-reviewed evidence. The project-specific conclusions come from the saved experiments, not from treating another paper's result as a prediction for MAESTRO.

| Source and publication | Where checked | Use and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Hoffmann et al., compute-optimal training](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.15556), NeurIPS 2022 | Sections 2–3; [conference record](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/c1e2faff6f588870935f114ebe04a3e5-Abstract-Conference.html) | Jointly consider size and training tokens. Section 2 discusses MoE and retrieval; it does not identify the best architecture for this project. Fresh-data/FLOP assumptions prevent direct use of its optimum here. The conference record uses the title “An empirical analysis of compute-optimal large language model training.” |
| [Muennighoff et al., Scaling Data-Constrained Language Models](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/9d89448b63ce1e2e8dc7af72c984c196-Abstract-Conference.html), NeurIPS 2023 | Conference paper, data-repetition results | Repetition has diminishing value in their language-model setting. This motivates studying diversity; numerical epoch thresholds cannot be transferred to overlapping MIDI windows. |
| [Huang et al., Music Transformer](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.04281), ICLR 2019 | Section 4.2, Table 3; [conference publication record](https://mlanthology.org/iclr/2019/huang2019iclr-music/) | Piano event modeling provides task-relevant support for relative attention and a precedent for pitch/time augmentation. Their tokenizer, architecture, tuning, and budget differ. Their losses cannot be compared numerically with ours, nor does their study isolate the gain our proposed augmentation would achieve. |
| [Fedus et al., Switch Transformers](https://jmlr.org/papers/v23/21-0998.html), JMLR 2022 | Published paper and abstract | Sparse experts add conditional capacity; complexity and stability matter. These results do not establish a single-A10 speedup or improved generalization for our small corpus. |
| [Srivastava et al., Dropout](https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/srivastava14a.html), JMLR 2014 | Mechanism and studied tasks | Random masking motivates the follow-up, not a promise that dropout 0.2 is optimal for every size. |
| [Loshchilov and Hutter, AdamW](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05101), ICLR 2019 | Decoupled weight-decay formulation | Explains why weight decay was another plausible follow-up. We did not test stronger decay, so cannot rank its effectiveness against dropout. |
| [Goodfellow et al., Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/ml.html), MIT Press 2016 | Chapter 5, especially Section 5.3 | Textbook grounding for overfitting and separating validation-based choices from testing. It is a textbook, not a new empirical result for this dataset. |
| [MAESTRO dataset documentation](https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/maestro) and Hawthorne et al., ICLR 2019 | Official split/format documentation and dataset paper | Supports dataset provenance and composition grouping. It is not an independent leakage audit performed by this project. |
| [The Smol Training Playbook](https://huggingfacetb-smol-training-playbook.hf.space/#why-the-question-nobody-wants-to-answer), Hugging Face, 30 October 2025 | “Why: The Question Nobody Wants to Answer” and ablation framing | Used only to justify defining a concrete research purpose before selecting model complexity. Practitioner guidance; not evidence that a recipe or architecture will improve these runs. |

Official [nanoGPT source](https://github.com/karpathy/nanoGPT) supports implementation provenance; [NVIDIA A10 specifications](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/products/a10-gpu/) support hardware descriptions. Neither is peer-reviewed validation of our results.

For reproduction rather than literature interpretation, use [reproduction.md](reproduction.md). The report bibliography contains the cited sources. Untested explanations and planned experiments are marked as such.
